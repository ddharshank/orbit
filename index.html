01 Materials & light

Surfaces that catch the light.

MeshPhysicalMaterial adds clearcoat and thin-film iridescence on top of standard PBR shading. Light comes from a prefiltered environment map made with PMREMGenerator, so reflections stay soft and physically plausible.

  • PBR
  • Iridescence
  • Environment maps

02 Instancing

1,024 columns. One draw call.

InstancedMesh sends thousands of copies of the same geometry to the GPU together. Each column gets its own matrix and colour every frame. Move your cursor over the grid to push it up.

  • InstancedMesh
  • Raycasting
  • Per-instance color

03 Post-processing

Make light bleed.

EffectComposer renders the scene to a texture first, then runs full-screen passes over it. An UnrealBloomPass picks out the brightest pixels and spreads them into a glow.

  • EffectComposer
  • UnrealBloomPass
  • HDR colour
